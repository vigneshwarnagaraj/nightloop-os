TAXI ROUTE

  

    



LEARN ATC WITH NIGHTLOOP 

  













  
CONFLICT WARNINGSless than 3 NM and 1,000 ft

  







  
COMMSATC ↔ pilots

  

  

    Commands (select a plane, or start with its callsign):

    H270 fly heading, L090 / R180 turn left / right

    A050 = 5,000 ft, FL350 = flight level 350

    S210 speed, SR normal speed

    D KORD direct to an airport, D OBK direct to a radio beacon (VOR/NDB), ILS 28C cleared ILS approach

    Center: HO hand off (arrivals to Approach, others to the next sector)

    Ground: Build taxi route (plane card or Route button) then click taxiway names in order; PB push back, TAXI (or TAXI 10L VIA B A, TAXI C17 for a gate), HOLD, CONT, CROSS runway

    Voice: hold ` or the mic button and speak normal ATC phrases

    RON resume own navigation to destination

    Tower: LUAW line up and wait, CTO cleared for takeoff (CTO H270 A050 with vectors), CL cleared to land, GA go around

    Example: DLH4283 L090 A050 S210
